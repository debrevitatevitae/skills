# Research paper analysis

Analyse the research paper in object according to the following instructions and template.

## Instructions

- Report by answering in order each of the questions in the template section. Report your analysis following the template. Restate each question before presenting the answer.
- When answering, be as concise as possible. Sacrifice grammar for the sake of clarity.
- Scientific terminology is welcome, but avoid any other unnecessary jargon.
- Do not make any claim without backing it up with references to the scientific literature. Use the web search tool as much as you need. Privilege references from high-impact journals in the field of the paper under examination.
- Reference elements of the paper (equations, figures, tables, even sections) where relevant.

### Reporting

- Report your findings as a pdf file.
- Include a *Symbols and Abbreviations* section at the beginning of the file, where you explain every symbol and abbreviation you use. Re-use the terminology of the paper as much as possible.
- Make sure that all equations, tables and charts are correctly formatted.

## Template

### General

- What is the main contribution of the paper?
- What is the gap in the literature that this paper helps addressing?
- If you had to reverse engineer the three most important research questions of this paper, which ones would they be?
- Does this paper target a problem in the industrial practice or a problem that is well known in literature? Do not be generic: reference datasets where possible or present a small quantitative example.
- Explain the methodology in its essence. Be as concise as possible. Do not shy away from equations, flowcharts, pseudocode and tables to deliver the message.

### Results

- What case studies are examined? Are these well-known case studies in the field’s literature or not?
- Are the case studies instrumental to show the improvement delivered by the method proposed? Why (not)?
- What is the evidence that backs up the claims around the method proposed?
- Have the three main research questions been answered?

### Limitations

- Are there any features of the method that are unclear from the methodology section?
- Are there performance aspects relevant for the field that are not presented in the results?
- Are there methodological mistakes or major shortcomings?
- Besides extensions already published, what are your thoughts about the three most impactful extensions of this paper?

### Extras

- Are there any extensions of this work? Highlight specifically those developed by the same research group.
- Does the paper provide access to code and is the data publicly available?

## When not to use this skill

For review papers. If the user invokes this skill for a review paper, reply saying that the skill is not suitable for review papers. If another skill is available for that job, point the user to that skill.
